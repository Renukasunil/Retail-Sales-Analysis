Retail Analytics

Executive view of sales, profit, products, stores, channels and inventory performance.

Reporting period: Jan 2021 – Oct 2025 · Inventory as of 31 Oct 2025

Executive summary

  • Electronics generated the highest sales and profit, far ahead of every other category.
  • Sales follow a clear yearly pattern: strong January–February, weakest September–November.
  • Dubai is the largest city by sales, followed by Abu Dhabi and Sharjah.
  • Stores are the largest channel; the website and mobile app together bring in a large share.
  • No product is currently below its reorder point, but many store-product pairs combine high sales with low stock.
  • Some products sell well but earn relatively low profit.

Management takeaways

  • Electronics carries the business. It is the top category for both sales and profit.
  • Sales are seasonal. Plan for strong Jan–Feb and weak Sep–Nov.
  • Dubai is the core market. Watch performance there most closely.
  • Stores lead, online follows. Both matter for revenue.
  • Watch stock on fast sellers. 2,098 store-product pairs combine high sales with low stock.
  • Check margins on 8 products. They sell well but earn relatively low profit.
